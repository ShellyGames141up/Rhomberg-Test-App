# Uncertainty budget

The calculation engine names every contribution and stores type, source, uncertainty, distribution, divisor, sensitivity coefficient and degrees of freedom. Standard uncertainty is input/divisor; the sensitivity-adjusted contributions are combined by root-sum-of-squares. Expanded uncertainty is combined uncertainty multiplied by the approved coverage factor. Effective degrees of freedom uses the Welch-Satterthwaite relationship where degrees-of-freedom data exists.

Temperature Type A uses the standard deviation of the UUT mean. Type B candidates include Reference Standard uncertainty/drift, source uncertainty/drift and UUT resolution. Pressure contributors must follow the approved method workbook and standard. External reliability links and template errors are unresolved and therefore block any claim of metrological approval.
