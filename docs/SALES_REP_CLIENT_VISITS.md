# Sales Representative client visits

The Clients tab is available in Representative mobile and desktop workspaces. Representatives see only customers assigned to their representative identifier. The page shows relationship activity, last verified visit, days since visit, next appointment, monthly RFQ/quotation/order counts, open work and green/amber/red visit health.

Representatives can schedule an appointment with date/time, expected duration, purpose, contact, address, notes, agenda, reminder and follow-up flag. Appointments are permanently linked to representative, customer, branch and visit history. Due-soon and overdue rules use the configurable visit cycle (safe mock default 30 days) and advance reminder (7 days). Email and push remain simulated/future only.

Verification uses multiple signals: appointment, consented geofence, customer confirmation or one-time QR, start/end times and duration. Representative-only confirmation is insufficient. Permission denied/unavailable location routes to the QR/customer-confirmation fallback.
