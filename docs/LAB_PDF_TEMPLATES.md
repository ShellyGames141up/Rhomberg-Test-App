# Laboratory PDF templates

The app generates three mock PDFs: internal calculation review, watermarked draft certificate and final unsigned certificate. They include Rhomberg identification, order/unit references, method/version, instrument identity, results, uncertainty, standards, generated date/by, classification and page numbers. Internal review additionally contains raw readings and management warnings.

Customer-safe certificates exclude internal notes, raw calculation evidence, audit metadata, management comments and private standard-maintenance details. Production templates require controlled branding, approved accreditation marks and wording, font embedding, PDF/A decision, accessibility review, stable pagination, cryptographic integrity and regression comparison against approved masters.
