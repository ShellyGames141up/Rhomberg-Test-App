# Branch Structure

Cape Town is Head Office. Port Elizabeth, Johannesburg and Durban Branch Managers see their own authorised branch customers, representatives, RFQs, orders, exceptions and reporting; they do not receive company-wide visibility from the role alone.

Laboratory work can route only to Cape Town or Johannesburg. Cape Town supports Pressure and Temperature responsibilities. Johannesburg currently supports Pressure technicians. Planning must select one of those approved Laboratory locations. Durban and Port Elizabeth cannot receive Laboratory jobs.

Branch transfers record an effective date, reason and active-responsibility impact snapshot. They never silently move customers, RFQs or orders.
