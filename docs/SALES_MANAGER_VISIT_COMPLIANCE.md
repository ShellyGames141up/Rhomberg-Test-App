# Sales Manager visit compliance

The Sales Manager Clients view reports assigned, visited, not visited, scheduled, completed, missed, overdue, average days between visits, average duration and compliance percentage by Representative. Drill-down evidence includes the customer, appointment, immutable verification history and approximate work-location summary.

Expired appointments without verification become `missed_visit`. The original event is retained when a Representative supplies a reason or reschedules. Location-derived statistics are approximate supporting information and must not drive automated employment or disciplinary decisions.
